Field Engineering Capabilities

Engineered for high reliability even in remote rural and industrial sectors.

📊

Phase Current & Unbalance

Captures real-time Red, Yellow, Blue, and Neutral phase currents. Computes load percentage against rated capacity and flags severe unbalance or high neutral current.

🛡️

Protection & Hardware Audit

Comprehensive checks for LT fuse ratings, MCCB condition, breather silica gel state, oil level & leakage, body earthing, and neutral earthing integrity.

📍

GPS Geo-Tagging

Instantly locks GPS latitude, longitude, and elevation with high-accuracy location services. Allows direct 1-tap Google Maps navigation to transformer poles.

👷

Surveyor & Inspector Log

Assigns and attributes reports to Junior Engineers, Line Supervisors, Linemen, or Agency Inspectors with sub-division and feeder details.

📤

1-Tap WhatsApp & Email Export

Generates formatted markdown inspection briefs with emoji status indicators for instant sharing to utility management WhatsApp groups or email.

📑

Comprehensive Excel / CSV Export

Exports complete multi-transformer survey databases into structured CSV/Excel sheets ready for DISCOM management reporting and planning.

How to Install on Android

Deploy directly to your smartphone or tablet in under two minutes.

STEP 01

Build / Download APK

Generate the release or debug APK from Android Studio, or download the latest release asset directly from your GitHub repository releases tab.

STEP 02

Enable Unknown Sources

On your Android phone, go to Settings > Security > Allow Installation of apps from your browser or file manager.

STEP 03

Install & Open

Tap the downloaded .apk file, click Install, and launch DTR SURVEY. All data will be saved locally on device.

STEP 04

Conduct Surveys Offline

Add new transformer inspections, capture GPS coordinates in the field, and export reports instantly to WhatsApp or Excel.
